Indulge yourself...

Indulge yourself at Viridian Salon, the best in the San Gabriel Valley. We only hire the best: our hairstylists are masters of their craft, creating looks ranging from demure and polished to vibrant and rebellious, curl or straight as a pin. Aside from haircuts and coloring we also offer a variety of hair treatments and spa experiences for every part of your head. We are dedicated to giving you an experience that leaves you feeling confident, comfortable, and rocking your favorite hairstyle ever.
